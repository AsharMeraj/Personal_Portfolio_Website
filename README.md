# 🌐 Personal Portfolio Website

This is a modern personal portfolio website built using Next.js. It is designed to showcase professional skills, projects, services, and background in a clean and structured way.

---

## 🚀 Landing Page (Hero Section)

The landing section is the first impression of the portfolio. It introduces the developer with a strong heading, a brief description, and a clear call-to-action such as downloading the CV.

<img src="public/assets/Landing.png" width="800"/>

---

## 🛠️ Services

This section highlights the services offered. It gives a clear idea of what the developer can deliver, helping potential clients or employers quickly understand capabilities.

<img src="public/assets/Services.png" width="800"/>

---

## 🧠 Skills

The skills section showcases technical expertise. It presents the technologies and tools the developer is proficient in, usually in a structured and visually appealing format.

<img src="public/assets/Skills.png" width="800"/>

---

## 💼 Projects

This section displays previous work and real-world projects. Each project typically includes a preview image and a short description, helping demonstrate practical experience and problem-solving ability.

<img src="public/assets/Projects.png" width="800"/>

---

## 📄 Additional Features

- 📥 Download CV functionality for quick access to professional details  
- 📚 Education section to highlight academic background  
- 🧭 Smooth navigation with a responsive navbar  
- 🧩 Dedicated work page for extended project or experience details  

---

## 🔄 Overall Flow

The website follows a simple and effective flow:

1. Users land on the Hero section  
2. Scroll through services to understand offerings  
3. Explore skills to evaluate expertise  
4. Review projects for real-world proof of work  
5. Download CV or navigate to detailed work sections  

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The main goal of this portfolio is to present a strong professional identity, showcase technical ability, and make it easy for recruiters or clients to evaluate and connect.
